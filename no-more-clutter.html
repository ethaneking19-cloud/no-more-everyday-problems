Why this problem sticks

Clutter happens when items lack dedicated drop zones.

Surfaces collect mail, keys, and receipts because taking them to another room takes extra effort. Setting up shallow open bins on counters gives every item an immediate target.

The 1-In, 1-Out Rule

Storage products create order, but keeping a flat surface clear requires a simple boundary: whenever you buy a new item, donate or recycle an older one.
