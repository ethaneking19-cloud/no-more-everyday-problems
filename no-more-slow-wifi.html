Why this problem sticks

Old ISP routers cannot penetrate thick walls or multiple floors.

Standard ISP-provided single routers struggle to broadcast through drywall, brick, and plumbing pipes. Placing mesh nodes in open central areas allows signals to bypass structural obstacles easily.

The Router Height Rule

Elevate your router or mesh nodes onto a shelf or desk rather than putting them on the floor or behind a TV. Elevated placement improves signal distribution by up to 30%.
