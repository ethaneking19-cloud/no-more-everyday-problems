Why this problem sticks

Clothes wrinkle when stored tightly packed or left in dryers.

Suitcases and overfilled laundry hampers crush fabric fibers. Steaming uses gentle heat and moisture to relax thread memory without damaging delicate fabrics like silk or wool.

The Shower Steam Hack

Traveling without a steamer? Hang your shirt on the bathroom door while taking a hot shower. The ambient humidity smooths out minor packing creases automatically.
