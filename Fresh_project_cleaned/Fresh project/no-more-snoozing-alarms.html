Why this problem sticks

Snoozing is caused by waking up abruptly in deep sleep.

Jolting awake to a harsh phone alarm forces your brain into "sleep inertia." Light therapy alarms gradually shift your body into light sleep phase so waking up feels effortless.

The Across-the-Room Phone Trick

Place your smartphone charging station on the far side of your bedroom. Standing up to silence your alarm breaks the physical inertia of staying in bed.
